BoxTune

You bought a subwoofer driver and the spec sheet hands you three cryptic numbers: Fs, Qts, Vas. Pick the wrong box volume and your build booms at one note or rolls off where the bass should start. BoxTune turns those three numbers into a real enclosure.

Design my box
  • Sealed designer - pick a target Q (0.71 for the flattest Butterworth response) and get the exact box volume, system resonance and -3 dB cutoff; or enter a box you already have.
  • Ported designer - Keele's published optimum volume and tuning, then the real Small 4th-order response of any volume and tuning you try: f3, f6, f10 and how big the boom peak is.
  • Response curve - see the bass roll-off and any hump before you cut a single panel.
  • Port calculator - diameter and count to physical length from the Helmholtz relation, with end correction.
  • Runs entirely in your browser. Four preset drivers included.

Small-signal Thiele/Small theory: sealed response is an exact 2nd-order high-pass; vented uses Small's 4th-order transfer function with leakage QL = 7 (a common published assumption). Keele volume/tuning suggestions are published approximations to Thiele's optimum alignments, and are labeled as such in the app. Real builds also depend on driver displacement, power and room - treat results as the proven starting point the formulas intend.
